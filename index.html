Visas — Both Households Headline focus

Since Brexit, UK nationals need a long-stay visa to live and work in Spain. The right visa is different for each of you. This page walks through all six relevant routes, then recommends per person.

The two-route picture: Three of you (Calum, Rose, Jack) most likely go via the Highly Qualified Professional (HQP) visa once you have a Spanish job offer. Mamina, as a self-employed founder, goes via the Digital Nomad Visa (DNV). Both routes attach spouses and children automatically and both unlock the Beckham Law 24% flat-rate tax.

Route 1 — Highly Qualified Professional (HQP) Calum · Rose · Jack

Spain's flagship route for skilled employees with a Spanish job offer. Closest equivalent of France's Talent Passport. The employer files the application with UGE-CE, then the visa is collected at the consulate.

RequirementDetail
QualificationDegree, postgrad, or 3+ years' relevant experience
Salary threshold — technical / scientific (art. 71)€40,077/yr gross
Salary threshold — managerial / executive€54,142/yr gross
Salary threshold — under 30€30,500/yr gross
Processing~20 business days (UGE-CE) + ~10 working days (consulate)
Initial validity3 years, then renewable
Family routeSpouse + children attached as accompanying family from day one
Tax benefitBeckham Law eligible — 24% flat-rate income tax up to €600k for 6 years

Step-by-step

  1. Secure a job offer at or above the relevant salary threshold
  2. Employer files HQP application with UGE-CE (Spain's central immigration unit)
  3. UGE-CE issues authorisation (~20 business days)
  4. You apply for the visa stamp at the Spanish consulate (BLS Spain in London / Manchester / Edinburgh) — ~10 working days
  5. Travel to Spain within 90 days
  6. Register at the town hall (empadronamiento)
  7. Apply for TIE (residence card) at the police station within 30 days; biometrics taken; card arrives 3–4 weeks later
  8. Family members apply alongside; one combined file

Route 2 — Digital Nomad Visa (DNV) Mamina

Launched in 2023 under Spain's Startup Law. For non-EU citizens working remotely — either as employees of a non-Spanish company or self-employed serving clients abroad. Spain-based client work must not exceed 20% of total professional activity.

For Mamina specifically: The DNV is almost certainly the right route. It preserves the UK Limited Co structure, keeps the option of UK NI continuity via CA3837, and unlocks Beckham Law. The only catch is the 20% cap on Spanish-source revenue — if Mamina's business is going to pivot toward Spanish clients post-move, the Autónomo Visa (Route 4) becomes the better fit.

Route 3 — EU Blue Card Jack (senior)

For highly-skilled workers earning above a higher threshold. The key benefit is intra-EU mobility after 18 months — useful for a PE professional who may want to rotate between Madrid, Frankfurt and London.

RequirementDetail
Salary threshold~1.5× average Spanish gross salary (~€55,000–€60,000/yr; updated annually)
QualificationDegree of 3+ years' study OR 5+ years' senior experience
Duration1–4 years, renewable
Key benefitIntra-EU mobility after 18 months

For a PE associate in their late 20s / early 30s, the HQP route is usually easier; for a VP / Principal-level PE role, the Blue Card unlocks better mobility — worth discussing with Jack's prospective firm.

Route 5 — Non-Lucrative Visa (NLV)

For those who can support themselves without working in Spain. Mostly used by retirees; usable as a backup for an accompanying partner not yet ready to apply on their own.

Route 6 — Job-Seeker Visa

Spain offers a 12-month visa for non-EU citizens looking to find work or set up a business. No job offer required at application. Backup route if any of you want to relocate before securing a contract.

Beckham Law — relevant to all four

Spain's Special Expats' Tax Regime (Beckham Law) lets qualifying new residents pay 24% flat-rate tax on Spanish-source employment income up to €600,000 for 6 years — instead of progressive rates up to 47%.

Key updates for 2026 (Startup Law refinements):
  • Digital Nomad Visa holders are now officially eligible
  • Spouse and children under 25 can be included from 2026
  • You must not have been Spanish tax-resident in the previous 5 years
  • Apply within 6 months of Spanish social security registration

Children — Mamina & Jack's son

The HQP, DNV and Autónomo visas all allow children to be attached as accompanying family members from day one. For a child under one:

  • Apply at the same time as the parents (one combined application)
  • Include the child's full UK birth certificate (apostilled and sworn-translated)
  • Comprehensive private health insurance covering the child
  • After arrival in Madrid, register the child on the empadronamiento with both parents
  • Children under 7 ride Madrid public transport free

Healthcare

Spain's SNS / SERMAS (Sistema Nacional de Salud, Madrid branch) is universal and free at the point of use once registered. Top European-quality care. About 25% of Madrileños also have supplementary private cover for faster appointments and consultant choice.

Path to access (employed via HQP)

  1. Employer registers you with Spanish social security
  2. You apply for Tarjeta Sanitaria Individual (TSI) at the local Centro de Salud
  3. 2–4 weeks: TSI in hand; primary care doctor (médico de cabecera) assigned

The headline tax-saver. 24% flat rate on Spanish employment income up to €600,000 for 6 years (instead of progressive 19–47%).

  • Spouse + children under 25 included (2026 update)

Pets & Family

Bringing two dogs from the UK; nurseries and British schools for a one-year-old; the family registration that unlocks everything else.

Moving two dogs from the UK Both households

UK pet passports are no longer valid for entry to Spain post-Brexit. Both dogs will travel under an Animal Health Certificate (AHC) from a UK Official Vet.

Required documents

  • ISO 11784/11785-compliant 15-digit microchip — must be implanted before the rabies vaccination, otherwise the vaccine is invalid for travel
  • Rabies vaccination given at least 21 days before travel
  • Animal Health Certificate (AHC) issued by an OV within 10 days of travel — replaces the old pet passport
  • Tapeworm treatment is not required for entry to Spain (only required for return to UK)

Timeline — start 8–12 weeks before

  1. Confirm microchip is ISO-compliant (replace if older)
  2. Rabies vaccination at OV — or check existing booster validity
  3. Book OV appointment for AHC, 1–10 days before travel
  4. Choose route (see below)
  5. After arrival: register the dog at the local Junta Municipal de Distrito; obtain Spanish EU Pet Passport (€30–€60)

Family registration — Empadronamiento

The empadronamiento (Padrón Municipal) is registration at the local town hall. It unlocks:

  • Public school place
  • Public nursery priority
  • Healthcare card (Tarjeta Sanitaria)
  • Madrid transport pass discounts
  • Many landlords accept the certificate as proof of address

Process: book a cita previa with the local Junta Municipal; bring passport, NIE, rental contract or property deed, child's birth certificate. Certificate issued same-day or 1–2 weeks later.
