# Job Market in Madrid — Evaluation, Health Data, Private Equity & Self-Employment
**Date researched: 2026-05-23**

## Overview

Madrid is one of Europe's growing professional hubs and the financial capital of southern Europe. For this group, the relevant ecosystems are:

- **International development & evaluation** (Calum) — UN agencies, WFP HQ, OECD-adjacent work, ECLAC, Spanish bilateral cooperation, big consultancies
- **Health data & research** (Rose) — ISCIII, ISGlobal, IDIBAPS-adjacent Madrid hospitals, IQVIA, Cegedim, pharma analytics
- **Private equity & financial services** (Jack) — Madrid has Iberia's largest concentration of PE and venture firms; growing as a southern-European Mediterranean alternative to London
- **Self-employment / consultancy** (Momina) — Spain's autónomo ecosystem is large; Madrid is the most international city to be self-employed in

Madrid is **cheaper than Paris or London** and significantly more English-tolerant than 10 years ago, particularly in tech, finance and international organisations.

## For Momina — Self-Employment & Consultancy

Momina is already self-employed — the question is whether to keep her business UK-based and serve clients remotely (→ DNV) or relocate the business to Spain (→ Autónomo Visa).

### Option A: Keep UK Limited Co, work remotely via DNV
- Maintain UK Ltd, continue invoicing UK clients
- Pay UK corporation tax and personal tax via the dividend/salary mix
- Keep CA3837 with HMRC to remain in UK NI
- Apply Beckham Law in Spain for the 6-year 24% flat rate on any Spanish-source income
- Suits any consultancy/services business with primarily non-Spanish clients

### Option B: Set up as autónomo in Spain
- Register with the Spanish tax authority (Hacienda) under Modelo 036/037
- Register with RETA (autonomous workers' social security regime); ~€80/month for year 1 under the *tarifa plana* (low-income discount), rising to ~€300/month
- Quarterly VAT returns (Modelo 303) and quarterly personal income tax (Modelo 130 or 131)
- Annual tax return (Modelo 100)
- Suits a Spain-facing business

### Hybrid options
- **Madrid coworking ecosystem** is strong: WeWork (multiple), Spaces (multiple), Impact Hub Madrid, La Industrial (Vallecas), Utopicus (multiple). Monthly hot-desk: €180–€300; dedicated desk: €300–€500
- **Autónomo lite** via remote freelancer marketplaces (Malt, Upwork, Toptal) is common

### Resources
- **ATA — Federación de Autónomos**: [ata.es](https://www.ata.es/) — main self-employed federation, can stamp business plans for Autónomo visa
- **UPTA — Unión de Profesionales y Trabajadores Autónomos**: [upta.es](https://www.upta.es/)

## Beckham Law — relevant to almost everyone here

If any of you become Spanish tax-resident on a qualifying visa (HQP, DNV, even Autónomo in some cases), the **Special Expats' Tax Regime (Beckham Law)** caps Spanish-source employment income tax at **24% up to €600,000**.

- Save up to €30,000+/year vs standard progressive bands
- Spouse and children under 25 can be included from 2026 under Startup Law refinements
- Must not have been Spanish tax-resident in the previous 5 years
- Must apply within **6 months** of social security registration
- Valid for 6 years

**Highly worth claiming if Jack lands a €150k+ PE role, or if Calum / Rose land €60k+ jobs in Madrid.**
