- **UPTA — Unión de Profesionales y Trabajadores Autónomos**: [upta.es](https://www.upta.es/)
- **UATAE**: [uatae.org](https://uatae.org/)
- **Asociación de Trabajadores Autónomos**: regional / sector chapters

### Co-working spaces with strong professional networks
- **Impact Hub Madrid** — Multiple locations; social-impact community
- **Utopicus** — Premium; multiple central locations
- **Spaces** — Global; multiple Madrid sites
- **WeWork** — Multiple Madrid sites
- **La Industrial** (Vallecas) — Maker / tech leaning
- **Talent Garden Madrid** — Tech and digital startups

### LinkedIn search strategies for self-employed work
- Search "freelance consultant Madrid" + your sector
- Join Madrid-specific freelancer groups
- Follow "AECOM, Tragsatec, EY, Deloitte freelance pools" — many big firms maintain rolling consultant rosters
