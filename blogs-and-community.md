# Blogs, Forums & Communities for British Expats and Professional Networks in Madrid
**Date researched: 2026-05-23**

## Overview

Three communities to plug into:
1. **British / anglophone expats in Madrid** — for daily-life support, friends, weekends
2. **Family / parent groups** — particularly for Mamina & Jack with a young child
3. **Professional networks** — PE & finance, evaluation, health data, autónomo

---

## British & Anglophone Expat Communities

### Forums & online groups

#### British Expats in Spain (Facebook)
- One of the largest UK-expat groups in Spain (~50k+ members)
- General Spain focus; useful for visa, healthcare, banking questions
- [facebook.com/groups/BritishExpatsInSpain](https://www.facebook.com/groups/BritishExpatsInSpain/)

#### Madrid Expats Group — Internationals in Madrid (Facebook)
- ~30k members; very active
- Daily life, recommendations, classifieds
- [facebook.com/groups/madridexpatsgroup](https://www.facebook.com/groups/madridexpatsgroup/)

#### Expats in Madrid (Facebook)
- Companion group; lots of recent-arrival questions
- [facebook.com/groups/Expats.in.Madrid](https://www.facebook.com/groups/Expats.in.Madrid/)

#### Expat.com — Madrid Forum
- Long-running Q&A community
- [expat.com — Madrid forum](https://www.expat.com/forum/viewforum.php?id=382)

#### Reddit
- **r/Madrid** — Active subreddit; many English-speakers
- **r/expats** + **r/GoingToSpain** — search "Madrid" for relevant threads
- **r/AskMadrid** — practical questions
- **r/spainfamily** — small but useful for families

#### British Expats Forum (general)
- [britishexpats.com/forum](https://britishexpats.com/forum/) — has active Spain section

### In-person and social groups in Madrid

#### British Hub Madrid
- Informal community of British professionals; networking events monthly
- LinkedIn group: search "British Hub Madrid"

#### British Society Madrid
- Older, formal British community; balls and tea parties; useful diplomatic connection
- Connected to the British Embassy in Madrid

#### InterNations Madrid
- Global expat platform; large monthly events at central Madrid venues
- [internations.org/madrid-expats](https://www.internations.org/madrid-expats)

#### Madrid Polyglot Club
- Language exchange + social meetups; mostly central Madrid
- Search Meetup.com / Facebook

#### Hash House Harriers Madrid
- "Running club with a drinking problem"; very British/Commonwealth heavy; weekly
- [madridhash.com](https://madridhash.com/)

#### Madrid Rugby Club / Cisneros Rugby
- For British/Commonwealth rugby community; teams for adults and juniors
- [madridrugby.com](https://www.madridrugby.com/)

#### British Embassy Madrid — community events
- Annual Queen's Birthday Party and other community events; useful contact point
- [gov.uk/world/organisations/british-embassy-madrid](https://www.gov.uk/world/organisations/british-embassy-madrid)

#### St George's Anglican Church
- C/ Núñez de Balboa, 43; English-language services; community noticeboard
- Strong cross-British/American expat hub

## Family & Parent Communities (Mamina & Jack's son)

#### MumAbroad Madrid
- The standard reference for expat parents in Madrid
- [mumabroad.com/madrid](https://mumabroad.com/mumabroad-spain/madrid/) — schools, healthcare, children's activities, classes
- Run by long-term Madrid expat mothers

#### Madrid for Kids
- Activities, schools, family events
- [madridforkids.com](https://www.madridforkids.com/) (Spanish-English bilingual)

#### English-Speaking Mums Madrid (Facebook)
- ~3k+ members; play dates, mum-friends, recommendations
- Search Facebook for "English Speaking Mums Madrid"

#### Madrid Mama
- Blog + community for English-speaking mothers
- [madridmama.com](https://www.madridmama.com/)

#### MyDaughterAbroad / British mums Spain (Facebook groups)
- Pan-Spain but Madrid-active sub-threads

#### Madrid International Parents Network
- Regular meetups at Retiro, Casa de Campo, indoor play centres
- Search Meetup / Facebook

#### Toddler & baby English-language groups
- **Babbery Madrid** — English-language pre-school + toddler classes
- **Cookies & Books** — English-language story-time at international bookshops
- **The Children's Hour** — English nursery + after-school
- **Music with Mar** — English music classes for under-3s

#### Paediatric / nursery directories
- **Sanitas English-speaking paediatricians** — list at Sanitas La Moraleja
- **British Hospital of Madrid** — paediatric department English-language

## Professional Networks — Private Equity / Finance (Jack)

#### CFA Society Spain
- The main professional finance network in Madrid
- Events, CFA prep groups, networking
- [cfaspain.org](https://www.cfaspain.org/)

#### ASCRI — Asociación Española de Capital, Crecimiento e Inversión
- Spanish private equity and venture capital association
- Annual conference; member-only events; data and reports
- [ascri.org](https://www.ascri.org/)

#### SpainCap (formerly ASCRI consumer brand)
- Member events; deal-flow gossip
- [ascri.org/quienes-somos/spaincap](https://www.ascri.org/quienes-somos/spaincap/)

#### IESE Business School Alumni Madrid
- IESE has a Madrid campus + huge alumni network; valuable for PE connections
- [iese.edu/alumni](https://www.iese.edu/alumni/)

#### IE Business School Alumni
- IE Madrid; another central PE / corporate finance network
- [ie.edu/alumni](https://www.ie.edu/alumni/)

#### London Business School Spain Club
- LBS alumni in Madrid; events and dinners
- LinkedIn group "LBS Spain"

#### Madrid Stock Exchange events (BME)
- Networking opportunities especially for IPO-adjacent PE work
- [bolsasymercados.es](https://www.bolsasymercados.es/)

## Professional Networks — Evaluation & Public Policy (Calum)

#### Sociedad Española de Evaluación de Políticas Públicas
- Spanish evaluation society (smaller than France's SFE)
- Annual conference; working groups
- LinkedIn search

#### EvalSDGs Spain
- Spanish chapter of global EvalSDGs

#### European Evaluation Society (EES)
- Biennial conference; many Spanish members
- [europeanevaluation.org](https://www.europeanevaluation.org/)

#### EvalCommunity
- Global M&E community
- [evalcommunity.com](https://www.evalcommunity.com/)

#### Devex Community
- Global development professionals network
- [devex.com](https://www.devex.com/)

#### Red Iberoamericana de Evaluación
- Latin American + Spanish evaluation professionals
- Useful for AECID/OEI connections

#### Madrid policy think tanks
- **Real Instituto Elcano** — foreign affairs; events open to public
  [realinstitutoelcano.org](https://www.realinstitutoelcano.org/)
- **CIDOB / EsadeGeo Madrid** — economics, geopolitics events
- **Fundación Alternativas** — progressive policy think tank; regular evening events

## Useful daily-life apps & services

- **Glovo / Uber Eats / Just Eat** — Food delivery
- **Cabify / Uber / Bolt / FreeNow** — Ride-hailing (Cabify is Spanish-origin and most reliable)
- **Wallapop** — Spanish Gumtree / Vinted; second-hand
- **Idealista** — Property, but also flatmates and short stays
- **EMT Madrid / Metro Madrid apps** — Transport
- **MyTaxi / Pidetaxi** — Traditional taxi-hailing
- **Coverflex** — Salary benefits platform some employers use
- **Doctoralia** — Find English-speaking GPs / paediatricians; book appointments
